G+4 Residential Building: seismic and wind design in ETABS

A five-storey reinforced concrete residential building, analysed for seismic and wind loads with a full 3D model.

The challenge

In a seismic zone, a mid-rise frame must stay stiff and ductile enough to protect occupants, while keeping columns and beams economical and coordinated with the architectural layout.

My approach

  • Built a 3D ETABS model with floor diaphragms, load patterns and mass sources.
  • Applied seismic loads to IS 1893 and wind loads to IS 875, and checked storey drift and torsional irregularity.
  • Designed beams, columns and slabs to IS 456, with ductile detailing for seismic resistance.
  • Prepared a Design Basis Report setting out assumptions, loads and methodology for review.
  • Sized footings from support reactions and coordinated column positions with the architectural plans.

Deliverables

  • ETABS model and analysis results
  • Design Basis Report (DBR)
  • Beam, column and slab reinforcement schedules
  • Foundation layout and details
