G+1 Coastal Villa: durable structure in an aggressive environment

A two-storey reinforced concrete villa close to the shoreline, where groundwater and salt-laden air drive every durability decision.

The challenge

Coastal sites in the UAE combine shallow, saline groundwater with chloride-rich air. Concrete that would last decades inland can start corroding in a fraction of that time here, so durability has to be designed in, not added later.

My approach

  • Modelled the frame in ETABS for gravity and lateral loads, and checked slab deflection and punching shear at columns.
  • Chose a raft foundation to spread load evenly over variable, water-bearing ground and to limit differential settlement.
  • Specified durability measures for the exposure class: increased concrete cover, a suitable cement type, a low water/cement ratio and protected reinforcement.
  • Detailed below-ground waterproofing and protection boards around the raft and ground-floor slab.
  • Coordinated with the architect and MEP engineers so openings and services did not weaken key structural members.

Deliverables

  • Structural model and design calculations
  • General arrangement and reinforcement drawings
  • Foundation and waterproofing details
  • Site support during inspections and concrete pours
