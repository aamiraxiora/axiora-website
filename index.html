About me

I'm Mohd Aamir, a civil and structural engineer who works on both sides of a structure: the design office and the construction site.

In design, I model and analyse reinforced concrete and steel buildings in ETABS and STAAD.Pro: villas, residential blocks, industrial sheds and institutional buildings, with seismic and wind analysis, Design Basis Reports and detailed drawings.

On site, I coordinate structural, MEP and architectural teams, manage shop drawings, submittals and inspections, and make sure what gets built matches what was designed.

Working in both places means my designs are practical to build, and my site decisions are grounded in engineering.
